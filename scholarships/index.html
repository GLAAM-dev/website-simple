Scholarships

The Mensa Foundation awards more than $150,000 in scholarship assistance every year to college-bound students around the world.

How it works

  • Awards are based solely on a 550-word essay about your academic and professional goals.
  • U.S. applicants do not need to be Mensa members.
  • College-bound Mensa members and their dependents may also be eligible for one of several $2,500 scholarships awarded each year.
Apply on the Mensa Foundation site

Who to ask locally

GLAAM Scholarships Committee Chair

Vacant

The local committee chair position is open. Applications go through the Mensa Foundation directly, so you do not need a local contact to apply. For chapter questions, reach the Local Secretary.
