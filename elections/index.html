Elections

GLAAM members elect the chapter's officers, area representatives, and members-at-large. Elections are run by the Elections Committee.

Who to ask

Elections Committee

Deputy: Jay Friedlander

Contact details are published in the L.A. Mentary newsletter.

If you cannot reach the committee, contact the Local Secretary on the Contacts page.

What gets elected

  • Executive officers — Chair (Local Secretary), Executive Vice-Chair, Administrative Vice-Chair, Secretary, and Treasurer
  • Area representatives — a Secretary and a Delegate for each of the six areas
  • Members-at-large — additional board seats

The current holders of every seat are listed on the Contacts page.

Where the rules live

Election procedures, terms, and eligibility are set by the chapter bylaws. Election notices and candidate statements are published in the L.A. Mentary newsletter.
