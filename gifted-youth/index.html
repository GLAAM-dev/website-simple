Gifted Youth

We schedule activities where children who are believed or diagnosed to be gifted can enjoy the company of intellectual peers their own age.

How it works

Gifted Youth activities appear on the regular chapter calendar. They are supervised by parent volunteers who have been specifically trained and evaluated.

Some history

Gifted youth work began as a Special Interest Group and became a standing committee around November 1968, when Bill Frambach was placed in charge of it.
