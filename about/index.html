About GLAAM

Greater Los Angeles Area Mensa (GLAAM) is a local chapter of American Mensa serving multiple counties across Southern California.

Our mission is to identify and foster human intelligence, encourage research into the nature of intelligence, and provide a stimulating intellectual and social environment for our members.

What is Mensa?

Mensa is an international society with one qualification for membership: a score in the top 2% of the general population on an approved intelligence test.

  • Founded in 1946 by Roland Berrill and Lancelot Ware.
  • A non-political and non-religious organization.
  • Over 140,000 members across 100 countries worldwide.
