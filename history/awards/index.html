About the awards program

In the summer of 2011 the GLAAM Board of Directors established a formal Awards Program for the purpose of recognizing those GLAAM members who provide volunteer service to the Mensa organization at all levels.  The Awards Program is designed to objectively track all of the volunteer service that members perform throughout their time as members of the Greater Los Angeles Area Mensa chapter.  A Standing Committee was established to carry on this work of maintaining and updating the information so that the program would be sustainable and ongoing as GLAAM heads into its future. There are two primary components of the Awards Program. The first seeks to recognize contemporary work from exceptional members.  The Member of the Year Award recognizes the individual who contributed the most to GLAAM during each award year.  The Rookie of the Year is a way of recognizing the energy and enthusiasm of a new GLAAM volunteer. The second component tracks the historical and cumulative contributions of our members over the years.  The Hall of Fame recognizes those members who have performed outstanding and sustained service for years while members of GLAAM.  The threshold for induction into the Hall of Fame requires the equivalent service of more than seven years serving as a LocSec, RG Chair, or Editor of the newsletter.  Beyond the Bronze level of the Hall of Fame, we also recognize service at the Silver, Gold, and Platinum levels, noting the additional contributions of those individuals who continued to serve Mensa in a variety of ways.  Many of these honorees are still contributing members of the GLAAM chapter, and we honor their continued work. From time to time, GLAAM has awarded a number of Certificates of Appreciation honoring significant contribution over a number of years. The Awards Program also acknowledges these recognitions.

Also see the Hall of Fame for cumulative lifetime service.

Member of the Year

The Member of the Year Award recognizes the individual who contributed the most to GLAAM during each award year.  Determination is made objectively, using a points schema which credits a certain number of points for each job, prorated as applicable for partial-year service, and adjusted when there is objective documentation of exceptional performance.
