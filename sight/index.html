SIGHT

SIGHT stands for Service of Information, Guidance, and Hospitality to Travellers. It connects visiting Mensans with local members who can offer advice, a meetup, and sometimes hospitality.

Visiting Los Angeles?

Contact our SIGHT coordinator before you travel. She can point you toward local events during your visit and put you in touch with members in the area you are staying.

GLAAM SIGHT Coordinator

Arlette Chew

Phone: (310) 973-8142

Email: SIGHT@glaam.us.mensa.org

While you are here

  • Events — see what is on the calendar during your trip
  • Special Interest Groups — regular dinners and discussion groups that welcome visitors
  • Regional Gathering — our annual weekend convention

Hosting travellers

Local members who would like to help visiting Mensans can volunteer through the SIGHT coordinator using the contact details above.
