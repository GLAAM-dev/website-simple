The Hall of Fame recognizes those members who have performed outstanding and sustained service for years while members of GLAAM.  The threshold for induction into the Hall of Fame requires the equivalent service of more than seven years serving as a LocSec, RG Chair, or Editor of the newsletter.  Beyond the Bronze level of the Hall of Fame, we also recognize service at the Silver, Gold, and Platinum levels, each of which requires the same amount of additional work which originally qualified the inductee for the Bronze level.  Many of these honorees are still contributing members of the GLAAM chapter, and we honor their continued work.  Many others are now deceased, and their permanent enshrinement here is a important way to remind us of and honor their legacies in perpetuity.
