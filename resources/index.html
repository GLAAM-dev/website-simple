Member Resources

Most member-only documents live on national Mensa systems. This page points you to the right place and explains what you will need.

Mensa Connect

Purpose: Official L.A. Mentary newsletter access, chapter financial records, meeting minutes, and private member forums.

You will need: An American Mensa membership login.

Open Mensa Connect
Looking for chapter files? Meeting minutes, financial reports, and other member-exclusive documents are hosted inside the Mensa Connect lounge.

Bylaws AI Explorer

Purpose: An interactive tool for exploring chapter bylaws using Google NotebookLM.

You will need: A Google account. This is an unusual tool — if you only need a PDF or official text, ask on Mensa Connect or contact the webmaster instead.
