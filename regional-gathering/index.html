StaRGazing 2027

GLAAM’s annual Regional Gathering

February 19–21, 2027 · Four Points by Sheraton, Culver City

Weekend convention — the weekend after Presidents’ Day. Speakers, games, entertainment, meals, and a kids track under one roof.

Register & full details on glaamrg.org Back to chapter events

Highlights

  • Speakers — keynote Dave Lopez and a full weekend of talks
  • Games — board, party, and card games all weekend
  • Meals — registration includes meals; vegetarian options available
  • Kids track — all-day Sunday program for gifted youth

Schedule, hotel block, speakers, FAQ, and registration are maintained by the RG committee at glaamrg.org.

Regional Gathering history

Since the 1970s GLAAM has hosted weekend conventions. The full chronology is kept in the chapter history archive.
