Events

Our live schedule is kept on Luma. It lists dinners, discussion groups, outings, and everything else on the chapter calendar.

Open the events calendar

You can sync the calendar with your phone so you do not miss anything.

Kinds of events

  • Special Interest Groups — regular dinners, book groups, and discussion groups
  • Gifted Youth — supervised activities for gifted children
  • Regional Gathering — our annual weekend convention
  • Meetings — board meetings that run the chapter

Regional Gathering: StaRGazing 2027

February 19–21, 2027 in Culver City, the weekend after Presidents’ Day. Speakers, games, entertainment, meals, and a kids track.
